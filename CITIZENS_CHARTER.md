### CITIZEN'S CHARTER
**EduScholar: Automated Tertiary Scholarship Management & Campus Aid Hub System**
*Compliant with Republic Act No. 11032 (Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018) and Quezon City Scholarship Program Ordinance (SP-2882, S-2019)*

---

## I. MANDATE, VISION, MISSION, AND SERVICE PLEDGE

### Mandate
The **Quezon City Youth Development Office (QCYDO)**, pursuant to City Ordinance No. SP-2882, S-2019, is mandated to democratize access to quality tertiary and vocational education for deserving Quezon City residents through competitive merit-based, economic-need, leadership, and specialized scholarship grants. The **EduScholar Portal (QC Campus Aid Hub)** operationalizes this mandate through a 100% digital, paperless, and transparent platform serving scholars, partner Higher Education Institutions (HEIs), and city administrators.

### Vision
A premier, digitally empowered local education support system where every qualified Quezon City youth achieves academic excellence, socioeconomic mobility, and professional success regardless of financial background.

### Mission
To provide prompt, transparent, inclusive, and equitable scholarship administration through modern digital workflows, automated verification, institutional partnerships, and streamlined financial aid disbursement.

### Service Pledge
We, the officials and staff of the Quezon City Youth Development Office, in collaboration with the City Treasury and Partner Academic Institutions, commit to:
1. **Promote Ease of Doing Business**: Deliver 100% online application, renewal, and credential evaluation without unnecessary physical queues or paper requirements.
2. **Zero Fees**: Provide all scholarship registration, evaluation, and endorsement services free of charge (₱0.00).
3. **Strict ARTA Turnaround Compliance**: Process simple requests within three (3) working days, complex applications within seven (7) working days, and highly technical or disbursement approvals within twenty (20) working days.
4. **Data Privacy & Security**: Safeguard citizen data, academic records, and financial disclosures under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).
5. **Zero Tolerance for Corruption**: Maintain an objective, merit- and need-based evaluation system backed by automated audit logging and verified registrar masterlists.

### SERVICE 1: Online Application for New Tertiary Scholarship Grants
*Intended for qualified Quezon City resident college students applying for tertiary scholarships (QC Excel, Tertiary Academic, Economic/Need-Based, Vocational/TVET, Postgraduate).*

| Field | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Office / Division:** | Quezon City Youth Development Office (QCYDO) – Scholarship Operations Unit |
| **Classification:** | Complex Transaction (7 Working Days) |
| **Type of Transaction:** | G2C (Government to Citizen) |
| **Who May Avail:** | Bonafide QC resident tertiary students enrolled in partner HEIs/SUCs/LUCs meeting scholarship qualifications |

#### Checklist of Requirements

| Requirement | Where to Secure |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1. Duly accomplished EduScholar Digital Application Form | EduScholar Portal (`/apply/scholarship` or `/e-scholar`) |
| 2. Certificate of Registration (COR) / Enrollment Assessment for the current semester | University/College Office of the Registrar |
| 3. Certified True Copy of Grades / Transcript of Records (TOR / COG) indicating GWA | University/College Office of the Registrar |
| 4. Proof of QC Residency (Barangay Clearance, QC Resident ID, or Voter's Certificate) | Barangay Hall / QC City Hall / COMELEC QC |
| 5. Proof of Household Income (ITR, Certificate of Indigency, or 4Ps Pantawid ID) | BIR RDO / Barangay Social Services / DSWD |
| 6. Track-specific Endorsement (Coach Letter for Athletic, Dean's Letter for Excel) | College Dean, Department Head, or Athletic Director |

#### Step-by-Step Procedure

| Step | Client Steps | Agency Actions | Fees | Processing Time | Person Responsible |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | Access EduScholar (`https://eduscholar.up.railway.app`), sign up or log in, and complete the Student Profile. | System automatically validates identity, QC residency barangay, and sets up encrypted student vault. | None (₱0.00) | 10 Minutes | Student Applicant |
| **2** | Select the desired scholarship program (`/apply`), fill out academic details, and upload digital PDF/WebP copies of COR, TOR, and residency proof. | System issues unique Application Tracking Reference (e.g., `APP-QC-178...`) and dispatches confirmation email/SMS. | None (₱0.00) | 15 Minutes | Student Applicant / EduScholar Automated Engine |
| **3** | Wait for institutional verification by University Registrar / Campus Coordinator. | Partner School Coordinator verifies genuine enrollment, unit load, and grades via the **Endorsement Desk** (`/school/endorsements`). | None (₱0.00) | 2 Working Days | Designated HEI Campus Coordinator |
| **4** | Track application status online through the Student Dashboard (`/dashboard`). | QCYDO Evaluator reviews submitted credentials, scores criteria, and forwards endorsed docket to Supervisor. | None (₱0.00) | 3 Working Days | QCYDO Scholarship Evaluator & Supervisor |
| **5** | Receive Notice of Award / Digital Grantee Certificate via email and portal notifications (`/notifications`). | System issues digital Notice of Acceptance and generates Scholar ID Barcode for disbursement. | None (₱0.00) | 2 Working Days | QCYDO Head of Scholarship Admissions |
| **TOTAL** | | | **₱0.00** | **7 Working Days** | |

## IV. FEEDBACK AND COMPLAINTS MECHANISM

In strict compliance with ARTA guidelines and the QC Citizen Care standards:

| Procedure | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **How to Send Feedback:** | 1. Fill out the digital **Feedback & Survey Form** available on the Scholar Portal (`/surveys`).<br>2. Submit remarks via the public Contact Page (`/contact`).<br>3. Send an email to `qcydo@quezoncity.gov.ph`. |
| **How Feedback is Processed:** | Feedback is collated daily by the Public Assistance Desk. Negative feedback or procedural bottlenecks are endorsed to the Division Chief for corrective action within twenty-four (24) hours. |
| **How to File a Complaint:** | 1. Submit an online complaint ticket via the **Help Desk Portal** (`/support`) specifying the Applicant ID, date, nature of grievance, and relevant evidence.<br>2. Call the **Quezon City Citizen Hotline 122**.<br>3. File directly with the **Anti-Red Tape Authority (ARTA)** at `complaints@arta.gov.ph` or via the Presidential Complaints Center (**8888**). |
| **How Complaints are Handled:** | 1. Complaints are acknowledged within twenty-four (24) hours of receipt.<br>2. An investigation report is submitted by the QCYDO Grievance Committee within three (3) working days.<br>3. Final written disposition is issued to the complainant within seven (7) working days. |
